# Media Blockers

## Vantage

The current first sequence uses the verified public homepage capture at `public/media/vantage/live-home.png`. It is sufficient for composition review but not for a final flagship trailer.

Required before final production approval:

1. A 12–18 second silent desktop recording at 1440px showing discovery entry, one meaningful browse/search interaction, and one trusted-result action.
2. A 9:16 mobile recording showing the same product path without cursor-dependent states.
3. Two clean interface crops: discovery results and saved/preference state.
4. Confirmation that the public deployment may be recorded and displayed in Affan's portfolio.
5. Final credit line for the team members whose visible interface work appears in the recording.

Capture rules:

- Use the public deployment only; do not stage fake product states.
- Hide personal accounts, tokens, locations, and private data.
- Record at native resolution, 30 or 60fps, no baked-in cursor halo, no audio.
- Keep team attribution adjacent to the final media caption.

## Current Fallback

Until those assets exist, retain the labeled static public capture. Do not substitute generic device mockups, stock imagery, invented dashboards, or AI-generated product screens.
