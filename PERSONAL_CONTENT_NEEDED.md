# Personal Content Needed

This list supports the Affan-first portfolio direction in `/design-lab/filippo-study/`. None of the requested material should be published until Affan supplies it, confirms the context, and approves public use. Team photographs also require permission from visible people where appropriate.

## Highest-priority photographs

1. **Candid portrait in a real environment**
   - Affan studying, building, walking in Toronto, at a competition, or in another personally meaningful place.
   - Avoid a centered studio headshot or a staged laptop pose.
   - Needed: original image, photographer credit, approximate date, location, and public-use approval.

2. **Hackathon or team-build moment**
   - A candid scene before a demo, during setup, at the work table, or immediately after the event.
   - Needed: event name, year, what Affan was doing, visible teammates, photographer, and permission status.

3. **Workspace with one odd or personal detail**
   - The real desk or study setup, including an object that says something about Affan outside software.
   - Needed: why the object matters and whether any private information is visible in the frame.

4. **Computational-biology object**
   - A biology book, microscope, diagram, notebook page, model, printed paper, or lab-related object Affan actually uses or cares about.
   - Needed: the object's name, why it matters, and a one-sentence connection to Affan's interests.

5. **Competition artifact**
   - Badge, lanyard, notebook, problem sheet, stage photograph, travel object, or post-event image.
   - Needed: competition name, year, Affan's role, the real story, and any award/result only if independently verified or confirmed.

6. **Life outside GitHub**
   - One photograph that would never appear in a repository README: hobby, sport, food, city moment, friends, family-approved scene, collection, music, or another genuine interest.
   - Needed: Affan's caption in his own voice and permission from anyone identifiable.
